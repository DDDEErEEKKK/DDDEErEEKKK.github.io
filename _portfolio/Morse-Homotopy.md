This document begins with a single question from Morse theory: when continuation maps, chain homotopies, and their higher analogues are assembled into a block differential $D$, why does the equation $D^2=0$ encode an entire hierarchy of compatibility relations? The first part is an English mathematical reconstruction of the original question and answer.

The algebraic part answers this through the dg-category of chain complexes. Mapping cones and homotopy-commutative squares appear as the first cases of a twisted complex, while the equation

$$
\delta q+q^2=0
$$

is the Maurer-Cartan equation in a filtered matrix dg-algebra. Resolving it by filtration degree produces chain maps, homotopies between their composites, homotopies between homotopies, and the subsequent higher coherence data.

The follow-up develops the geometric questions suggested by that answer. It relates the hierarchy to compactified Morse trajectory spaces, coherent framings, relative Pontryagin-Thom maps, and the Cohen-Jones-Segal realization of the Morse flow category. It also examines how smashing with $H\mathbb Z$ retains the ordinary Morse differential while forgetting higher stable attaching information, and how fundamental chains of trajectory spaces form a geometric Maurer-Cartan element.

The discussion culminates in further questions about stable enhancements of filtered $H\mathbb Z$-modules and the geometric recognition of framed flow categories arising from genuine Morse-Smale systems.
